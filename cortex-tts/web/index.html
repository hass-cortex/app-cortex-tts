Cloned voices

cloning models

One recording becomes a voice on every model that clones, under the same id.

  • Six to ten seconds of clean speech that starts straight away. Under 2 s or over 20 s is refused.
  • End on a finished sentence. A clip cut mid-word is refused.
  • Type exactly what is said. A wrong transcript degrades the clone with no error.
  • Chinese goes in Simplified. Traditional glyphs are misread. For a Taiwan reading, write a homophone that forces it: 系阳 for 夕阳.
  • Tag the language whole. zh-TW gets Taiwan readings by default; zh promises only Chinese.
